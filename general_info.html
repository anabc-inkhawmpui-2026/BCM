General Information

Inkhawmpui chhunga hriat tur pawimawhte
1

Palai lokal te reception a in report pahin badge leh report & agenda bu lak zel nise.

2

Mikhual te mutbu keng remchang lo leh sa ei theilo te kan awm chuan, reception ah kan in report zel dawn nia.

3

Inkhawm apiangin thawhlawm lakkhawm ani ang.

4

Inkhawm leh Rorel tan hun a hnuaia tarlan ang hian ruahman a ni.

Zan lam inkhawm tan hun – 7:00 PM , 6:30 PM atangin Pathian faka zaiho tur a ni
ROREL TAN HUN – 10:30 AM
Pathianni zing inkhawm tan hun – 10:30 AM
Inrinni tlai ah Rorel palai leh zaipawl ten ruai min kilsak turin kan sawm vek a che u.
5

Motor keng tan Biak In bulah motor dahna tur parking a awm a. Lirthei dah tuten Traffic duty te ruahmanna kan ngaichang zel dawn nia.

6

Zaipawl ten Sound i/c te enlawkna hun siamah enlawk tum nise, harsatna a awm chuan insiamrem zel tum a ni ang.

7

Inkhawm ban chhuah dawn ah Usher duty te ngaichang hram hram turin kan in ngen e.

8

Nuamsam lo kan awm chuan medical duty te Hall no.2 ah a rawn theih e.

9

Inkhawmpui programme te, thlalak leh information tul te AN ABC inkhawmpui webpage or QR code ah en leh download theih a ni e.
